# Nutrition & Obesity Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

## Objective
Analyse lifestyle and dietary habits alongside obesity levels to find patterns across gender, age group, transportation, family history and eating habits.

## Dataset
Excel dataset of 2,084 people provided during the Data Science and Analytics internship at Imarticus Learning.

## Approach
1. Loaded and cleaned the data in Power BI using Power Query
2. Created a new BMI column using Power Query
3. Built KPI cards: total people, average BMI and obesity percentage
4. Created a two-page interactive dashboard covering obesity levels and lifestyle factors

## Key findings
- Average BMI is 29.77, and 46.64% of people fall into one of the three obesity categories.
- Obesity Type I is the largest group (351 people, 16.84%).
- Obesity Type II is almost entirely male (295 of 297), while Obesity Type III is almost entirely female (323 of 324).
- Nearly all people with obesity report a family history of overweight (only 8 of 972 do not).
- About three-quarters of people use public transportation, the dominant mode of transport.
- Most people (1,463 of 2,084) eat three main meals a day.
